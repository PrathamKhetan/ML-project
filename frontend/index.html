Heuristics

Transparent AI Decision Matrix

Every point added by the risk engine corresponds to an auditable corporate rule.

Threshold Exceeded +50 Points

Any expense exceeding the $10,000 corporate ceiling automatically flags for executive review.

Duplicate Submission +30 Points

Exact match on employee name, category, and monetary amount against previous filings.

Off-Hours Timing +20 Points

Submissions filed on a Saturday or Sunday, or between 11 PM and 5 AM overnight.

Category Outlier +25 Points

Monetary amount exceeding 300% (3x) of the historical category baseline.

Unitemized Round Number +10 Points

Suspicious flat integer amounts (e.g. $5,000, $2,500) without cent breakdowns.
